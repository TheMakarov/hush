All 128 blocks have NVDM headers. Offline parsing validated 4,937 records,

This validation does not demonstrate that restoring the store would work.

Interesting key names include optimizer_coeff, nc_is_optimized,
touch_panel_disabled, test_forced_touch_sensor, sound_position, voice_language,
power_saving, codec_ldac, codec_aptx, and codec_aptx_hd. Those names are facts
about storage. Their meaning, mutability, and useful behavior are not proven.
In particular, generic aptX keys do not establish working aptX support.
Four CXD90050 ALIGN_* trim keys and DNC/howling_pre_notice_off are present.
No raw calibration or test setting was modified.

The backup includes device identity and pairing records. It stays local with
mode 0600 and is excluded from Git. The UI never exposes their values, and the
HTTP server cannot serve the raw backup directory.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR FIRMWARE EXTRACTION
Confirmed here: reading the logical NVDM settings/calibration store.
Not tested here: a complete physical flash, bootloader, or running firmware dump.
A single physical-flash page-zero read (storage_type=0) was attempted and
refused by the headset with status 0x03. We did not enter update mode or try
to bypass that restriction. Logical NVDM reads still succeeded independently.
Do not treat a 512 KiB NVDM backup as a full firmware recovery image.
The research repository reports offline decryption/decompression of downloaded
official firmware images; that is separate from extracting this device's flash.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR FLASHING / “BURNING”
The community toolkit below reports real WH-1000XM4 flashing in both directions
between official 2.5.0 and 3.0.1 images. Its flasher allowlists known official
images by SHA-256 and uses the device's firmware-update path. This is a
maintainer report; no firmware write or downgrade was performed on your unit.
Sony's currently listed 3.0.1 release improves security. A downgrade can remove
security fixes, and interrupted or unsuitable writes can make the headset unusable.
Before an actual flash, establish the exact image, model/region compatibility,
hash, power/link conditions, and a recovery route; NVDM alone does not provide one.

Arbitrary custom firmware installation is not established by the evidence we
reviewed. Experimental persistent ANC coefficient injection is disabled upstream
on real hardware because its storage assumptions were contradicted by dumps.
Claims that a downgrade improves ANC are subjective reports, not measurements.

READ-ONLY SCOPE OF THIS INVESTIGATION
Used: SDP discovery, PAGE_READ on logical NVDM, and one rejected physical-flash
page-zero PAGE_READ. The physical flash test discarded any returned data.
Not used: FOTA update-mode handshake, flash erase/program, RAM writes, test-mode
toggles, microphone capture, calibration changes, or reboot commands.
