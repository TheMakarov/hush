Sony MDR payload semantics were reverse engineered by Gadgetbridge;
framing was cross-checked by upstream against SonyHeadphonesClient (MIT).
Hush adds the loopback HTTP bridge, confirmed readback, interface, diagnostics,
read-only storage probe, and tests. It uses Python's standard library only.

RACE/NVDM framing and storage-layout research:
https://github.com/randwardatake/wh1000xm4-anc-optimizer
https://github.com/randwardatake/wh1000xm4-anc-optimizer/blob/main/tools/backup_nvdm.py
https://github.com/randwardatake/wh1000xm4-anc-optimizer/blob/main/docs/HARDWARE_AND_NVDM.md
The probe validates observed reply metadata and implements no write commands.
