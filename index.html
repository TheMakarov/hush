WHY DISCORD SOUNDS LIKE A PHONE CALL

Stereo music and the XM4 mic use different profiles.

The XM4 uses A2DP for high-fidelity stereo playback. Opening its microphone causes the Linux audio policy to select HFP/HSP, a call profile with much less audio bandwidth. An app’s 48 kHz processing format does not restore the Bluetooth link’s lost fidelity.

Inspecting audio policy…

To keep the good sound: select a separate USB, webcam, or built-in microphone as Discord’s input, and keep the XM4 as its A2DP output. The headset’s own microphone needs the call profile on this Classic Bluetooth connection.
